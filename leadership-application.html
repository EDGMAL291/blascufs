Before You Apply

Please read the eligibility requirements carefully. Applications open during the third term of each academic year.

  • Must be a member in good standing
  • Must have been active for at least one academic year (for Chairperson)
  • Must not be under any legal disability
  • Nominations must be seconded by two members in good standing
  • All applications are subject to BEC review
