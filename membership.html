Membership

Join BLASC UFS

Open to all registered UFS law students. Join for R50 annually and become part of a movement that is transforming the legal profession.

Eligibility

Who can join

Membership is open to all registered UFS students who are studying towards a law qualification (LLB, BA Law, BCom Law, Postgraduate Law) and who accept the BLASC constitution, policies, and principles.

Leadership Development

Build governance capability through chapter roles, committee work, and student leadership initiatives.

Networking

Connect with fellow students, alumni, legal practitioners, and partner institutions.

Programmes & Events

Access Gear Up, NPA Shadowing, Moot Court, career workshops, and legal awareness programmes.

Mentorship

Learn within a supportive network of peers, senior students, and experienced legal mentors.

Vacation Work

Access to BLA-facilitated placements at law firms, the NPA, and government departments.

Your Voice

Contribute to social justice advocacy and be a voice for law students at UFS and nationally.

How to Join

  1. Create an account or sign in with a verified UFS student email address.
  2. Complete the membership profile form.
  3. Submit your student number and academic details for BEC review.
  4. Pay the annual membership fee of R50 using the banking details provided on confirmation.

Membership Fee

Amount: R50 per year
Banking details will be provided upon confirmation of your membership application by the BEC.

For payment queries, contact Treasurer Alakhe Mvuyu via Ufsblascu@gmail.com

Membership Account & Application

Create your account first. Your membership will be reviewed by the BEC after your details and payment reference are submitted.

After submitting your details, the BEC will review your application and confirm your membership once payment is verified.

Student access only Accepted login emails must use a UFS student domain. Personal Gmail, iCloud, and other public email addresses should not be accepted for membership accounts.

Use your UFS student email or student number.
