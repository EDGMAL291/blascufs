NPA Partnership

In the Shadow of the Prosecutor

A formal court-shadowing programme connecting UFS law students to the practical world of public prosecution through collaboration between the National Prosecuting Authority, the UFS Faculty of Law, and BLASC UFS.

Back to Programmes

Programme Overview

This flagship programme was developed to bridge legal theory and prosecutorial practice. Students gain guided observational exposure to courtroom preparation, case processes, professional ethics, and standards expected in formal criminal justice environments.

Application Details

  • Applications open: 1 May 2026
  • Maximum: 15 students per week
  • Submission does not guarantee placement
